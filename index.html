Withdrawal rules
Minimum withdrawal: 10,000 DCX.
Maximum withdrawal: No limit, subject to your available DCX balance.
Request limit: Only 1 withdrawal request in every 24-hour period.
Processing time: Within 24 hours after a valid request is submitted.
Manual processing: your request will be reviewed by the DCX admin and paid to your verified TON wallet within 24 hours. Payment is sent manually from the DCX Payout Wallet.

Your verified TON wallet will be used as the withdrawal destination. Never share your seed phrase or private key.
